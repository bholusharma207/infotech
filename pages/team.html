Our Team

At Pointer Educational Trust consists of passionate professionals and industry experts dedicated to empowering students with real-world skills. They bring practical knowledge, mentorship, and support, creating a learning environment where students can confidently pursue meaningful careers—all within their own communities. Together, we're committed to building brighter futures through accessible, quality education.

Mr. Ramesh Chandra Maurya

Director of Education and Faculty / Trustee President

Mr. Ramesh Chandra Maurya, a distinguished educator, has dedicated his life to nurturing and shaping young minds with unwavering commitment and care. With decades of teaching experience, he has mentored countless students, guiding them toward success while instilling values that inspire lifelong learning and social responsibility.

His experiences in teaching and his passion for serving society inspired him to establish the Pointer Educational Trust. As the Founder and Trustee of the trust, he has expanded his mission beyond the classroom to serve underprivileged communities. Leveraging his extensive expertise in education, he works tirelessly to ensure that quality education and opportunities reach those in need. Driven by a profound sense of responsibility and a vision to empower individuals, strengthen communities, and foster a culture of learning and growth, his efforts stand as a testament to his unwavering dedication. By combining professional acumen with a passion for social welfare, he continues to make a transformative impact, contributing to the creation of a more educated, equitable, and progressive society.

Managing Director / Vice President

Digvijay Maurya has always been passionate about helping others. From a young age, he actively contributed to various NGOs, working across different sectors to support community welfare. Despite facing financial challenges in his education, he received invaluable support from Asha Trust Mumbai, which enabled him to continue his studies. This experience deeply inspired him to give back to society. After working with the trust for several years, he resolved to establish his own educational trust to support others facing similar struggles.

After completing his education, Digvijay gained valuable experience in the corporate sector. Driven by a desire to make a meaningful impact, he joined and took a leadership role at Pointer Educational Trust. His mission is to empower individuals by sharing his knowledge and skills, helping them build a better and more sustainable future.

Mr. Vikas Kumar

Treasurer / Secretary

Vikash Kumar, a B.Sc. graduate from Kanpur University, faced early challenges in his career but overcame them by pursuing a diploma in Computer Hardware and Networking. This opened the door to his first job, marking a pivotal moment in his career. Inspired by his journey, Vikash decided to help others facing similar struggles and founded "Pointer IT Infotech Organization" to empower individuals and create job opportunities.

His mission is to reach as many people as possible and help them build better futures. By collaborating with Pointer Educational Trust, he aims to expand his efforts and positively impact even more lives. His journey is a testament to the transformative power of patience, education, and empowerment. Through his work, he continues to guide others toward success and a secure future.
