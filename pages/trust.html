Pointer Educational Trust

-Way to Brighter Future

Pointer Educational Trust is an NGO focused on empowering underprivileged youth in rural areas through affordable digital education. We offer tech-driven programs, including computer literacy and professional training, along with a self-learning space featuring access to PCs and expert mentorship to help students succeed in high-demand fields.

Our mission

At Pointer Educational Trust, our mission is to transform lives by providing accessible, high-quality digital and technical education to underprivileged youth, regardless of their location or background. We aim to equip students with in-demand skills and practical experience through programs such as Basic to Advanced Computer Courses, Professional Skills Training with Spoken English, and more. In addition, we provide a dedicated self-learning space with access to PCs and expert mentorship. By bringing these opportunities directly to rural communities, we help students learn, grow, and achieve their full potential right where they are.

Our vision

Our vision is to bridge the educational gap in underserved regions by delivering affordable, career-focused technical training within local communities. Along with training, we strive to provide a self-learning space featuring access to PCs and mentorship, empowering students to continuously learn and practice on their own. We aim to help students build successful careers without the need to migrate to distant cities. We envision a future where every student has access to the tools, skills, and guidance required to thrive in today's competitive job market—creating a more skilled, confident, and self-reliant society.

Our Projects

At Pointer Educational Trust, our projects are designed to bring practical, skill-based education and career opportunities to underserved communities. Each initiative reflects our commitment to empowerment through technology and learning.
